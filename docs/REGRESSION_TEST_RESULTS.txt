SteamOS package and isolated profile
  PASS   Built the Flutter Linux release and generated a fresh AppImage
         payload. The new AppImage extracted successfully. Its AOT payload
         was byte-identical to the built bundle. Embedded version.json
         reports 0.16.1+2135. ldd reported no missing runner dependencies.
         libflutter_chd.so's direct dependencies are libm and libc.
         Evidence build/integration-delivery-release.log and
         build/integration-delivery-package.log.
  PASS   Steam Deck desktop startup in extract-and-run mode. The process
         remained alive for the 35-second smoke and was stopped by the test
         timeout. A second launch opened a visible 1280x720 window. The
         application detected the Deck's controller. The final AppImage
         also started in normal FUSE mode on the Deck and rendered a
         visible 1280x800 window until the 25-second timeout.
         Evidence build/integration-steamos-smoke.log,
         build/integration-steamos-restart.log and
         build/integration-delivery-smoke.log.
  PASS   Started and restarted against a SQLite snapshot of the user's
         library with startup scanning and updates disabled on the copy.
         The 1,415 ROM rows, 38 detected systems, 2 ROM directories,
         14 emulator settings and 10 per-system settings retained their
         counts. ROM path, owner, title, descriptive metadata, favorite,
         hidden, history and emulator override values compared equal.
         Manual RA associations compared equal. The GameCube policy
         changed to gamecube/hash_only and cleared legacy automatic
         hashes in the snapshot. The schema remained 188.
  PASS   Dolphin 2609b computed rcheevos hashes for all 20 indexed
         GameCube RVZ images. The local RA catalogue identified 19 as
         having achievement sets. This verifies frontend hash evidence.
         Dolphin's local RA token was empty, so emulator sign-in and
         in-game award eligibility were unavailable to verify.
